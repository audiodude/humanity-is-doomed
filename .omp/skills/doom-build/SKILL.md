---

Turn a design doc (`levels/designs/<module>.md`, from `doom-design`) into `levels/<module>.py` that builds clean and matches the doc. No doc yet: run `doom-design` first. Playtesting is `doom-test`.

## 1. Read

- `README.md` "The level builder" through "Code layout": the format, every check, the room helpers.
- `levels/mapgen.py` docstring, `levels/model.py` (`Sector`, `Special`, `Thing`, and the helpers), `levels/doom2.py` (thing types, line actions, sector specials).
- One existing module for syntax: `levels/nukage_junction.py`. Take its syntax only; the layout comes from the doc.

## 2. Write and build

Module header: `MAP = "MAPnn"`, `WAD = "<NAME>.WAD"`, `UMAPINFO` with `levelname`, `"author": "Generated by Claude for tmoney"`, `next` (omit on the last map), a stock DOOM II `music` lump. A docstring with the run command, like the existing modules.

Build into a scratch WAD while iterating, so other maps in the real WAD stay as they are:

```sh
python3 -c "import levels.<module> as m; from levels import build; build(m, out='/tmp/<module>.wad')"
```

`python3 -m levels <module>` writes the real `wads/<WAD>` (replacing only this map's slot) and its preview; run it once the level is finished.

Fix until there are no `ERROR` lines. Warnings are allowed when the reason is understood (a vanilla texture that can't tile a short wall, say).

## 3. Compare with the doc

- Read the preview SVG (`read` with `:img`) next to the doc's sketch. Every space in the route is there, with the heights the doc gives, and the shape reads as the doc's concept rather than boxes off a corridor.
- Read the measurement table the build prints. Each of the doc's targets is met, or the miss is written into the doc's Targets section with the reason.
- Check rule-1 slips (floor texture change with no height change) listed by sector pair; fix those that aren't door thresholds.
- Check the wall textures against the doc's materials table: every sector's `wall`, `lower`, door faces and alcove walls come from its area's row, and the texture metrics (most-used wall texture, rule-2 border share) meet the doc's targets.
- Fix the rule-2 seams the build lists (two textures meeting in one wall plane with no border texture), each at the coordinates given: use one material on both sides if the change has no reason in the doc; otherwise put a trim between them, as a door track, an alcove in the trim texture, or a short wall line (a vertex either side) given the trim in `OVERRIDES`. A switch or door face set straight into a material wall counts too; frame it.

Done when the build is clean, every route space is in the preview, every target is met or its miss recorded in the doc, and every wall texture is in the materials table.

## Gotchas

Observed in past builds; most cost an agent several rebuilds to find.

- **Shared vertices**: lines meet only at shared vertices. A room edge that a neighbour's corner touches mid-segment needs that vertex in its outline too.
- **Doors**: door pictures (`BIGDOOR2` and kin) are 128 tall and must fill their wall, so a door between rooms taller than 128 needs `doorway(..., alcove=, far_alcove=)` recesses 128 high on both sides. `far_alcove` extends past the door; start the next room after it. A sky courtyard on one side pushes rooms out 32-48 units for its recess.
- **Monsters stuck at doors**: in vanilla (and the 2024 re-release), a monster blocked by a wall while its box crosses a plain `DR_DOOR` line "opens" the door and counts the step as taken, so it never turns, and it pushes into the wall forever. Wall continuing in the door's own plane makes this far more likely, so `doorway()` recesses are exactly door-width (id's shape): don't hand-build a wider one, and a door side with no recess sits flush in its wall with the same problem. That halves the freezing; only `action=D1_DOOR` (opens once, stays open; monsters can't use it) ends it, and D1 changes how the room plays. The playtest bot can't show it (ZDoom randomises its way out). Measurements and videos: `docs/door-stuck.md`.
- **Stairs**: steps 32 deep left the top unreachable to the progression check; 48-deep steps passed. Steps taller than 24 pass the build but stop the playtest walker ("walk stuck").
- **Pedestal**: `pedestal()` doesn't cut its own hole; add its rect as a hole in the surrounding sector, or its trap trigger fails with "no shared edge".
- **Exit niche**: a 32-wide `SW1EXIT` niche is too narrow for the player in the reach check; leave it out of `MUST_REACH`.
- **Lifts**: the reach check treats a lift at its top height, so the space it lowers into needs a ceiling at least 56 above the lift top. build() adds walk-over `WR_LIFT` from the top, which monsters can trigger and ride; override it in `SPECIAL_BETWEEN` with a use action when that matters.
- **Rising floors**: the raise actions move at 0.5 units per tic (a 96 rise is about 5 s) and stop at the next higher neighbouring floor, so start a bridge close to its final height and keep neighbouring floors from catching it.
- **Open spaces**: your first shot floods every sector joined by an opening (railings too), and a deaf monster (`"a"` in `Thing.skills`) that hears it wakes as soon as it has any line of sight to you, whichever way it faces. Deaf monsters across an open space from you wake together and gather below you. Keep a group asleep by cutting its sound off with two sound-blocking lines in series (`ML_SOUNDBLOCK` in `OVERRIDES`); `docs/monster-alerting.md` has the rules and MAP09's dam-and-islands pattern.
- **Pits**: any drop the player can't climb out of without a lift needs stairs or a lift back, or a railing, unless the doc makes it a deliberate one-way drop.
