# Spoilers

Walkthroughs for the maps in `wads/NUKAGE.WAD`. Play them first.

## MAP01: Nukage Junction

Route: tech start room → courtyard hub with a nukage pool → warehouse (lift to a ledge switch that lowers the blue-key island; the fake wall in the west wall leads to the secret) → blue door → lab (lift to the red key; grabbing it opens a monster closet) → red door → exit arena (ambush closets on entry) → exit switch.

## MAP02: Nukage Crossing

- **Set piece:** a 128-high sluice gate stands in a nukage canal. A switch in the silver control tower lowers it into a footbridge, and you can watch it sink through the window beside the switch. The pinkies pacing the far yard then charge across.
- **Route:** start window with a view across the canal to the red door and a soulsphere on a lit pedestal → south yard → dark blinking pipe corridor → bright pump hall, up a step to the blue key (grabbing it opens a closet behind you) → blue door → north bank → control tower → across the bridge to the sealed south-east yard (red key; grabbing it opens closets between you and the bridge and a guardroom by the red door) → back across → terrace → red door → exit. A switch in the south-east yard opens a gate back to the start, which closes a loop through the start yard.
- **Secrets:** a start-room closet behind a shifted panel holds armor. A lit canal ledge, reached by wading in from the pump hall's intake, holds the rocket launcher. A seamless tower panel opens onto a pier and a lift down to the soulsphere island; the only hint is the pier and island visible outside. A shifted panel in the filter house hides a backpack.
- **Gag:** a switch sits in a niche in the south-east yard's east wall, right by the parapet. Press it and the piece of parapet beside it drops away, and the only thing left to do is walk into the canal, which teleports you back to the switch.

## MAP03: Nukage Core

- **Idea:** a goal you climb toward. A reactor core stands in a nukage moat in an open-air atrium, with the exit in the crown on top. You see it from the start window, then spiral up around it on three galleries at rising heights, each looking across at the core and down at the ground you've covered.
- **Route:** start window → terrace → dark pump room → west gallery (super shotgun) → tank room, where grabbing the blue key blacks the room out and opens two closets while the floor's nukage pools keep glowing → blue door → stair tower → north gallery → turbine hall, where the first mancubus waits on a dais with imps in its line of fire and taking the yellow key from behind it sinks the walls of two pens → yellow door → stair tower → east gallery → control room, whose switch beside a window raises the shutter across the causeway → onto the core, which opens the crown and its last mancubi → exit. The same switch opens a lift from the east gallery down to the terrace, closing a loop through the start.
- **Optional:** a filter house off the terrace, a pier over the moat, and a storage room whose crate stack you climb to a berserk pack.
- **Secrets:** each is marked by a shifted panel, except the lift. A filter-house panel hides a plasma rifle. A lift out of the moat climbs to a ledge on the core's south face, in view of the start window (blue armor, rockets). Panels in the pump room (green armor), the turbine hall (backpack) and the north-east stair tower (soulsphere).

## MAP04: Nukage Divided

- **Idea:** crossed keys you can see. Leaving the start you face a wall that is half lava, half blue computers, with a red furnace wing (red torches, flickering firelight) to the left and a bright blue cooling wing (blue torches) to the right. The wings never meet. The hot wing ends at the red key and a locked blue arena door; the cold wing at the blue key and a locked red door. Each antechamber's window looks across a nukage pit at the other key between its torches. A switch in each antechamber opens a shortcut back to the fork, so finishing one wing sends you straight into the other holding the key it needs.
- **Hot wing:** a corridor walled with fire → the furnace hall, around a nukage moat and a furnace brimming with nukage behind lava walls; taking the super shotgun from its pedestal opens two fire-lit closets → antechamber, where the red key opens a closet behind you. Optional: stairs up to a loft with a window into the arena. Imps, demons and spectres up close.
- **Cold wing:** the coolant hall (chaingun) → the server room, among rows of computer racks → stairs up to the antechamber, where the blue key opens a closet. Optional: a lift down to the dark sump and its nukage pools, where a second super shotgun sits on a spotlit pedestal; taking it turns the lights on, and the room is full of hell knights and chaingunners. Hitscanners at range.
- **Arena:** the hot door enters at floor level, the cold door onto a balcony above two barons (a baron and a hell knight on Hurt Me Plenty). Coming in either way opens closets on both sides. The exit switch is on the dais behind the barons.
- **Secrets:** a shifted panel in the hot fork and a lift down to a soulsphere island in the pit; a loft panel (rocket launcher); a server-room panel (backpack); a crate stack in the coolant hall (green armor).

## MAP05: Nukage Drain

- **Idea:** the plant is flooded, and you pump it out. You start on the only dry catwalk of an open-air reservoir full of nukage, with the exit vault inside a pillar in the middle. The walkways around the reservoir and the causeway to the vault are under the sludge. Each of three pump houses has a switch beside an opening onto the reservoir; throw it and the next walkway rises out of the nukage, dry, leading to the next pump house.
- **Pump fights:** each pump switch is at the back of a booth, and stepping into the booth opens a closet in the face of the pillar you're looking at, and cacodemons and lost souls fly out across the nukage at you, through the opening beside the switch.
- **Route:** south catwalk (a closet off it holds the radiation suit) → west pump house (dark pipework; taking the super shotgun from its pedestal opens two closets, and stairs climb to a gallery) → pump 1 raises the west walkway → north pump house, where the first revenant waits alone on a ledge and the yellow key opens two closets → pump 2 raises the north walkway → yellow door → east boiler house (rocket launcher) → pump 3 raises the east walkway, which leads back to the start, and the causeway to the vault. Stepping onto the causeway opens two closets on the east walkway behind you. The exit switch is in the vault.
- **In the nukage:** a soulsphere in the far corner behind the pillar, a berserk pack, a chaingun, two rocket boxes, and a trail of armor bonuses round the pillar.
- **Secrets:** a lit ledge on the pillar's north face, out of sight until you wade round it (blue armor, rockets); shifted panels in the west corridor (green armor), the north lobby (shells, a medikit) and the east boiler hall (backpack).

## MAP06: Nukage Undercurrent

- **Idea:** one hall crossed twice, over it and then under it. The start booth looks down a railed gantry along the turbine hall's north wall to the blue door at the far end; from the balcony beside it you look straight down at the yellow exit door.
- **Set piece:** the yellow key sits on a red-lit cradle at the end of the generator gallery. Reaching for it sinks the cradle and its parapet 160 units into the well, where demons and spectres are waiting, and a door at its foot opens onto the boiler room. There's no way back up.
- **Route:** start booth → gantry (super shotgun on the balcony) → the gantry is broken halfway, so: annex corridor → control room, blue key on the console (taking it opens two closets in the ring corridor behind) → back onto the gantry past the break → blue door → generator gallery round the well → the cradle → boiler room (rocket launcher, a revenant on its dais) → dark pipe gallery (chaingunners) → boiler passage, whose door opens into the hall floor; stepping in lets the first arachnotron out onto the gantry above you → the floodgate blocks the floor: sump → switch booth, whose barred window shows the gate sinking → across the gate, which lets the second arachnotron out above the exit and opens the balcony lift → past the mancubus closet → yellow door → exit vault.
- **Optional:** the switchgear vault off the annex, the loft over the generator gallery, the pump room (berserk), the cooling yard (megaarmor on an island in a nukage pond), and the turbine half of the hall floor.
- **Secrets:** a lit ledge high in the hall's south wall, in view from the start, reached through a shifted panel in the sump's east wall, a corridor and a lift (soulsphere); a server room behind a lit panel in the annex's west corridor (backpack); a panel in the loft (plasma rifle); a lit alcove at the back of the nukage pool under the gantry break, seen from the control-room window (blue armor).

## MAP07: Nukage Storage

- **Idea:** one hall in two states. The plant's spent-fuel store is a maze of steel casks; you see it whole from a gallery, walk it aisle by aisle to the control tower, and the tower's switch sinks every cask into the floor at once. The maze becomes an open hall with four columns standing, and the six mancubi sealed in cells among the casks are suddenly in the open, facing the cab window. There's no exit switch to find: DOOM II's own MAP07 rule drops the dispatch shutters, under the EXIT sign you've seen since the gallery, when the last mancubus dies.
- **Route:** guardhouse window over the yard (flatcar, truck, the store's gate) → yard → lockers (super shotgun) → dark decontamination corridor → stair and landing → corridor back south → the gallery (chaingun), where the first pain elemental comes off the tallest cask at you through open air → down the stair into the maze: north under the gallery, east, north, east to the blue-lit cask stack → up it to the yellow key, which opens two closets in the north wall behind you (a baron and imps) → south and east to the yellow door → tower base (rocket launcher) → tower stair → the cab: the switch beside the window sinks the casks, and drops a closet wall on the stair behind you (a revenant and imps) → the arena: kill the six mancubi → shutters → the dock (arachnotrons among the crates) → exit vault.
- **Optional:** a switch in the maze's south aisle opens the yard gate, closing a loop through the start; that aisle also leads to the cooling pond yard (cacodemons over the pond, megaarmor on the island). The records office loops from the landing to the gallery's north end. The supplies on the cask tops (the megasphere, rockets, cells) come down with the casks.
- **Nukage:** the flatcars have leaked along the yard's rail bed; a drain gutter runs along the foot of the gallery; every mancubus cell holds the pool its cask leaked, so the arena has six of them once the casks sink; and a trench crosses the dock, with an arachnotron standing in it. All of it hurts.
- **Secrets:** the cask on the yard's flatcar has a SILVER3 hatch that sinks when used (backpack, a box of shells, a box of bullets); a panel in the records office's north wall, shifted beside a light strip (rocket launcher, a whole maze early); a shifted panel at the maze's north-east dead end, with a light strip beside it (plasma rifle); a shifted screen in the cab's east wall onto the tower roof, whose blinking beacon and soulsphere you can see from the cooling pond yard (soulsphere).

## MAP08: Nukage Lockdown

- **Idea:** a loop away from the exit you can see. You start in the lobby looking through a window at the exit switch, on a lit console under an EXIT sign, in the vault next door; the lobby's only open door leads away from it. The plant's storage tank has burst into an open-air basin in the middle of the plant, and the route runs right round the basin through the buildings on its banks, and closes by wading back through it.
- **Lockdown shutters:** grey SPCDOOR3 shutters behind a blinking red lamp open only from their far side, by a switch. Each one you open is a shortcut back.
- **Route:** lobby → ready room → gallery over the basin (chaingun), cut by a shutter → from the top of the lift, a running jump onto the lit pump column with the super shotgun; taking it drops the column, and you, into the dark pump pit, beside a demon and a spectre waiting there (the lift takes you down too, without the gun) → pit stair, where halfway up a hell knight comes out onto the landing above → the gallery's north half, where a switch opens the shutter behind you → tank hall (rocket launcher; a mancubus by the blue door, a revenant on the tank) → up the tank stair and round its rim to the blue key beside the rupture, where green falls pour into a channel; taking the key opens two closets in the hall (an arachnotron and imps) → blue door → filter house, among tubs of nukage (a baron) → east bank (radiation suit; an arachnotron, a mancubus, cacodemons over the basin) → wade west along the basin to the culvert → decon hall: the arch-vile on its dais, with fodder round the pool to raise → the exit vault. Crossing into it opens the closets in the lobby you started in, and two revenants shoot at you through the window. A switch in the vault opens the lobby shutter; another in the airlock off the decon hall opens the shutter onto the east bank.
- **Optional:** the north annex off the tank hall, the filter house's loft (berserk, rockets), the sampling shed, the airlock, and the island in the basin, with the megasphere you can see from the start window.
- **Secrets:** a shifted panel in the lobby's south wall (backpack, green armor); a pump plinth faced in SILVER3 in the pit is a lift up to a lit ledge (megaarmor); a shifted screen in the north annex's west wall, a lift up to the lit alcove high in the tank hall's north wall, in view from the rim (plasma rifle); a shifted panel at the back of the sampling shed (soulsphere).
